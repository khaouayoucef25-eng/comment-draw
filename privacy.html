Comment Draw Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 · Operated by Algerian Modern Distribution Pharmaceutical

In short: Comment Draw reads the comments on a post from a Facebook Page you manage, picks a random winner in your browser, and shows it to you. It stores nothing on any server, sells nothing and shares nothing.

What Comment Draw does

Comment Draw is a giveaway tool for Facebook Page administrators. After you log in with Facebook and choose one of your Pages, it reads the comments on a post you select and picks one or more random winners. It shows the winner's public name, profile picture and comment so you can announce the result.

Information we access

  • From you (the Page administrator): the list of Pages you manage and a temporary access token for the Page you choose (pages_show_list).
  • From your Page: your Page's posts and videos so you can choose one (pages_read_engagement).
  • From people who commented on your post: the comment text, comment time, comment link, and the commenter's public name and profile picture (pages_read_user_content).

Comment Draw only reads. It cannot post, comment, reply, like, send messages, delete content or change anything on Facebook.

How we use it

The information is used for one purpose: to pick a random winner from the comments and show the result to the Page administrator who started the draw. It is not used for advertising, profiling, marketing or any other purpose.

Storage and retention

  • Everything is processed in your own web browser. Comment Draw has no database and no server that receives Facebook data.
  • The access token and the comments are kept only in the memory of the open browser tab. They are erased when you press "Log out", reload the page or close the tab.
  • The only thing saved in your browser is the app's public ID, so you don't have to type it again.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. Facebook data is sent only between your browser and Facebook (graph.facebook.com).

Data deletion

Because Comment Draw stores no Facebook data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To remove Comment Draw's access to your account:

  1. On Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  3. Select Comment Draw and choose Remove.

If you commented on a Page post and want to ask about a draw, contact the Page that ran it. You can also delete your comment on Facebook at any time.

Children

Comment Draw is meant for Page administrators and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
