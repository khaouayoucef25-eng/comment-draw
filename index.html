Comment Draw

Paste the link of a post from your Facebook Page. All comments are collected quietly and one random winner is revealed. The comment list is never shown.

0One-time setup

Facebook only lets apps read comments after you log in through an app you own. Create a free app once (about 10 minutes), then paste its App ID here. Full steps are in the README file next to this page.

1Connect your Page

Your Page isn't in the list? Facebook only shows the Pages you ticked when you connected.

2Choose the post

Short share links (facebook.com/share/…) can't be decoded by Facebook's API. If yours isn't found, pick the post from your recent posts.

3Rules and draw

Separate with commas. A comment that contains any of them counts.

How this page keeps you safe
  • It only asks Facebook for read-only access. It can't post, comment, message or change anything.
  • Your login key stays in this tab's memory. It is never saved, and closing the tab or pressing "Log out" removes it.
  • It runs on your own computer (localhost) and can only talk to Facebook. No other website, tracker or server is involved.
  • The winner is picked with your browser's cryptographic random generator.
  • To remove the app's access at any time: Facebook → Settings → Business integrations.
